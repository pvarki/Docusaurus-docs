![](./_media/87651291-16b2-4528-9492-f49ab41ef59b.png "=216x") The Bloodhound Tool provides support for tracking and intercepting a map item. It allows for the selection of two points on the map, and/or map objects, and displays Range & Bearing information between the chosen tracker and the target.
