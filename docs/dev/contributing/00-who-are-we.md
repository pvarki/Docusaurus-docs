### What?

We do open source tech for Finnish & Western defence & security purposes**.** We want enable troops get using software products they need, in an ad-hoc, yet fast, easy & secure way. 

* __KPI: Battle-ready in 30 minutes from pulling the trigger, with completely untrained personnel.__

### Why?

**Tactics** - the need in the battlefield. Situational Awareness, Instant Messaging and various other services are force multipliers. They make troops to act multiple times faster & proactively. That will multiply the impact of their capabilities.

**Logistics** - such products don't appear out of nowhere. We strive to deliver soldiers & security operators **more, better, with less.** 

### **How?**

We create **core open source products**, that should aid to get other products to the hands of Soldiers. 

We aim make our stack **well integrable** so that **you can integrate your product to it** and get it to troops aswell. Permanently licensed permissively, one of our top priorities is to enable you to deliver your solution to military users. When you can build upon us, we're hitting our target.

We create **an open source community t**hat can consistently create high quality and secure products. The access to the community is not public, but public open source contribution and usage is appreciated. 

### Goals

With such measures we aim to create an ecosystem of effective & modern defence software. Single vendors won't win the day. When we can collaborate without central control, the innovation that delivers may begin.
